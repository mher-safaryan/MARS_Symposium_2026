## Location & Venue

### Reaching the campus from Lancaster city centre

- **By public transport:** Several buses run on a regular basis between Lancaster and campus, including the lines 41, 42, 1A, 4, and 100. Journey time is approximately 15 min (41, 42, 1A) and 25 min (4 and 100), respectively. The line 4 directly connects the train station to campus, whereas the other bus lines operate from the nearby city centre.

- **By taxi:** There is a taxi rank at the station, but if one is not available there are several local taxi services, e.g.:
  - 848 848 Radio Taxis — 01524 848848
  - 32090 Taxis — 01524 32090
  - Coastal Taxis — 01524 60000
